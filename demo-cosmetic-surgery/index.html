Your questions, answered

What happens at the first consultation?

You meet your surgeon privately, discuss your goals and medical history, and receive honest advice about options, risks and recovery. There is no obligation.

How long is recovery?

It varies by procedure. We give you a realistic timeline for time off work, activity and when to expect final results.

Will my results look natural?

That is our aim. We plan conservatively around your proportions and discuss what is realistic for you.

Is my privacy protected?

Yes. Consultations are private and your information and photos are handled confidentially.

Can I discuss payment options?

Of course. We provide a clear written estimate and can talk through the options available.
