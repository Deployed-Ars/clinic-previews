Questions, answered

How often should I visit the dentist?

Most people benefit from a check-up and clean every six months. We'll recommend a schedule based on your oral health.

Is teeth whitening safe?

Professional whitening is safe when done after an exam. We check your gums and enamel first.

Does treatment hurt?

We use modern anaesthesia and gentle techniques so you stay comfortable. Tell us if you feel anxious and we'll go slowly.

How long do dental implants take?

It depends on your bone health and the plan, often a few months from placement to the final crown. We'll give you a clear timeline.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We welcome children and aim to make the first visit relaxed and fun.

How do I book?

Call us, send a WhatsApp message or use the request form on this page and we'll confirm a time.
