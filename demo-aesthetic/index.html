Frequently asked questions

Do I need a consultation first?

Yes. A consultation lets us recommend what truly suits your skin and goals, and explain results, downtime and cost.

Are laser treatments safe for darker skin tones?

With the right device and settings, yes. We select technology suited to your skin tone and test where needed.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the treatment and your skin. We'll give you an estimate at your consultation.

Is there downtime?

Many treatments have little or none. We'll tell you what to expect and how to care for your skin afterwards.

How do I book?

Use the form below, message us on WhatsApp or call, and we'll confirm a time.
